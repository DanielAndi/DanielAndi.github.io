Privacy policy

Danordy Studio. Last updated November 20, 2026.

Who uses it

Danordy Studio is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer, for his own YouTube channel. It has no other users and no website or server of its own besides this page.

What it accesses

When its owner signs in with Google, Danordy Studio asks for these YouTube permissions:

  • upload videos to his channel and manage them (YouTube Data API: youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl, used for uploads, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publish times);
  • read his channel's videos and settings (youtube.readonly);
  • read his channel's statistics, such as views and watch time (yt-analytics.readonly).

Where the data goes

The sign-in token and everything read from YouTube are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to anyone else, sold or shared, and nothing is used for advertising. The data is used only to upload and schedule his videos and to see how they perform.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access page; deleting the stored token on the computer removes the Studio's copy.

Google API Services

Danordy Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
