Danordy Studio

Danordy Studio is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It edits his gameplay recordings into videos and uploads them to his own YouTube channel, scheduled to publish later.

It signs in to YouTube with Google only to upload those videos, set their titles, thumbnails, playlists and publish times, and read the channel's own videos and statistics. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else signs in to it.
